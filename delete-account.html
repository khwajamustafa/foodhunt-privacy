How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your FoodHunt account, send us an email with your request. We will process your request within 7 business days.

  • Send an email to our support team at the address below with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include your registered phone number in the email body so we can identify your account.
  • We will send you a confirmation email once your account has been successfully deleted.

Before You Delete

Consider these alternatives before permanently deleting your account:

  • You can request that we correct or update your data instead.
