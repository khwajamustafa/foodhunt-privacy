Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FoodHunt ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the FoodHunt mobile application.
01

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use FoodHunt:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and phone number provided during registration.
  • Location Data: Your approximate or precise location to show nearby restaurants, cafes, and food suppliers.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, including searches, clicks, and viewed listings.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the FoodHunt service.
  • Show you relevant restaurants, cafes, bakeries, and food suppliers near you.
  • Send OTP verification codes during registration via your phone number.
  • Personalize your experience and remember your preferences.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform.
03

Firebase & Third-Party Services

FoodHunt uses Firebase (by Google) for authentication and data storage. Firebase may collect certain data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

04

Location Information

FoodHunt requests access to your device's location to show food businesses and suppliers near you. Location access is optional — you may skip location permission and still use the app. We do not share your precise location with third parties.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure.

06

Children's Privacy

FoodHunt is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time via your device settings.
  • Delete your account by contacting us.
08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of FoodHunt after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
